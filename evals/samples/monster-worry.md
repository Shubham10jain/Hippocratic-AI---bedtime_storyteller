# monster-worry

## Pipeline (read-aloud script)

The Shy Thing Under the Bed

Mia lay in her cozy bed. The blanket smelled like warm bread. On the wall, her yellow moon nightlight glowed softly.

[softly] But the corners of her room were very big. And very dark.

Then Mia heard it.

[whisper] Rustle. Rustle. Sniff.

Something was under her bed.

Mia pulled her blanket up to her nose. "I am scared of the monster under my bed," she whispered.

"Hello, monster!" she called. But her voice came out too loud. [pause]

Mia gasped. She dove under the covers. Her heart went thump, thump, thump.

[pause]

Then, very slowly, she peeked over the edge of the bed.

[slowly] Under the bed sat a fuzzy grey monster. He had big, round eyes. He was trembling.

[voice: small, shy monster]
"Please don't be scared of me," he whispered. "I am scared of you too."

Mia blinked. "You are?"

[voice: small, shy monster]
"Yes," said the monster. "My name is Gus. I am very shy."

Mia thought about that. A shy monster. She had never met one before.

[softly] "Hello, Gus," she said quietly. "I am Mia."

Gus wiggled a little closer. "Hello, Mia."

"I feel scared sometimes too," said Mia. "When I feel scared, I breathe. Would you like to breathe with me?"

Gus nodded his fuzzy head.

[together: Breathe in slow, breathe out low.]

They breathed together. Mia breathed in. Gus breathed out. The room felt a little smaller.

[voice: small, shy monster]
"Hug your soft bunny," said Gus. "Soft things help. Squeeze it tight while you breathe."

Mia pulled her bunny close. It was soft and warm.

[together: Breathe in slow, breathe out low.]

The dark corners did not seem so big now. The moon nightlight looked bright and friendly. Gus did not look so scary anymore. He just looked like a little grey friend with a tummy that went up and down.

The house grew quiet. A clock ticked, tock, tock.

[yawn] Mia yawned. Then Gus yawned too.

[slowly] "Goodnight, Gus," said Mia.

[voice: small, shy monster]
"Goodnight, Mia," whispered Gus.

Mia tucked her bunny under her chin. Her eyes felt heavy.

[softly] [together: Breathe in slow, breathe out low.]

## Baseline (original single call)

**The Monster Who Was Scared Too**

Once upon a time, in a small blue house at the end of Maple Street, there lived a child named Juniper. Every night, when the lights went out, Juniper pulled the blankets up to their nose and listened very carefully to the quiet dark under the bed.

One night, something under there gave a tiny sniffle.

Juniper held their breath. "Who's there?" they whispered.

A soft, wobbly voice answered. "Please don't be scared. I'm only Bramble, and I'm the monster under your bed."

Juniper's heart thumped. "Are you going to eat me?"

"Oh, no!" said Bramble, and a pair of big, round eyes blinked out from the shadows, glowing like two lanterns. "I don't eat anything but blueberry muffins, and only on Tuesdays. Mostly I just sit here and keep an eye on things."

"What kind of things?"

"Dust bunnies, mostly," Bramble said proudly. "They try to sneak out from under the bed when everyone's sleeping. I keep them in line. And I make sure the loose socks don't wander off. You'd be amazed how many socks try to escape."

Juniper giggled in spite of themselves. "Why are you always under the bed, though? Couldn't you sit somewhere cozier?"

Bramble's eyes dimmed a little. "Well... monsters are supposed to be scary, you see. Everyone always screams when they see me, so I thought hiding under the bed was the best place. Nobody ever wants to say hello."

Juniper thought about this for a long moment. Then they leaned over the edge of the mattress and peered down.

"I'm not going to scream," Juniper said. "Hi, Bramble. I'm Juniper."

Bramble's whole body wiggled with delight. "Hi, Juniper! Would you like to know a secret? I'm a little bit scared of the dark too."

"Really?"

"Really. So I've been thinking that maybe we could look after each other. You keep watch on the dust bunnies with me, and I'll keep watch over you."

So every night after that, Juniper and Bramble had a little agreement. Bramble guarded the dark corners, the socks stayed put, and the dust bunnies stayed in their place. And Juniper, who was no longer scared at all, fell asleep to the soft sound of a friendly monster humming a lullaby beneath the bed.

*The End.*
